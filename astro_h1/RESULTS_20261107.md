# XAUUSD H1 & astrology: reproducible 2020-2026 results, 2026-11-07

Actual archived Exness XAUUSD Raw Spread quotes: **295,560,873**, 2020-01-29 through 2026-10-30. **39,184** complete Monday-Friday UTC H1 BID candles; **28,593** train (through 2024), **10,591** test (2025-Oct2026). Derived at the H1 FIRST quote: Swiss Ephemeris Lahiri/geocentric planet longitudes, 15 major planet pairs and aspects, mean Rahu/Ketu, Saturn/Mars/Jupiter traditional graha-drishti proxies, nakshatra/tithi/yoga/karana, planetary retrogrades, London sidereal ascendant, astronomical Rahu Kalam/Yamaganda/Gulika/planetary Hora for London, Colombo and New York. **Not full personal birth-based Vimshottari Dasha or Sade Sati.**

## Results
- Strong repeatable hourly volatility pattern: **13:00 UTC H1 0.5331% average high-low range** vs **04:00 UTC 0.1785%**, ratio **2.99x**. The expected human London/New York market session and economics release schedule confounds all celestial/solar-time effects.
- London Rahu Kalam H1 overlap (4,424 candles): mean range **$10.1517** vs non-overlap **$7.9197** (+28.18% raw). Hour/week/month/year adjusted coefficient p≈**0.5139** (clustered by ISO calendar week), not independent evidence. Colombo Rau Kalam raw range $7.4446 vs $8.2606; New York $8.5835 vs $8.1220. City-dependent signs suggest time-zone aliasing.
- Nakshatra 27 H1 volatility raw Kruskal-Wallis p≈6.5e-8; adjusted week-cluster omnibus p≈**0.4640**. Tithi 30 p≈1e-7 naive; adjusted p≈**0.2751**. Saturn retrograde raw p≈4.38e-49; adjusted p≈**0.0822**, and 2025-2026 alone p≈**0.8572**.
- Annual same-date same-hour H1 candle direction: **49.279%** same-direction among **25,108** cross-year pairs, actual Pearson signed-return correlation **+0.0080**. Same weekday/hour 52 weeks earlier: **50.071%** same-direction among **32,909** pairs (Pearson r≈−0.0022).
- 2025-2026 same calendar day/hour contrarian replay: **8,174** signals, **51.26%** raw directional match, yet **−$150.79** idealized net after archived actual Bid/Ask and 0.01-lot round-trip fee $0.07.
- Chronological test with 17 predeclared model families:
  * No-astro calendar/market baseline **AUC 0.50527**, H1 close-open dollar MAE **$8.01206**.
  * Solar-only **AUC 0.51748**, directional accuracy **51.893%**, close-open MAE **$7.99741**, but confidence filter yielded zero trades; no-new-change MAE **$7.99888** (difference negligible).
  * Market plus Rahu/Ketu-Saturn 23 features **AUC 0.52387** (Δ+0.01860), MAE **$8.02970** (worse for exact close), directional accuracy **50.10%**. Idealized entry/exit first/last tick 0.01 lot net +$391.90 across 5,448 trades, **2025 −$234.47; 2026 +$626.37**. Additional execution cost of ~$0.072 per signal erases modeled net.
  * Market plus ALL astrology **AUC 0.4917**, MAE **$8.5227**, idealized net −$1,993.28.
- Independent local reproduction of baseline probability matched published GitHub run max error 2.3e-14. Weekly-block bootstrap 700 repetitions for nodal-versus-baseline AUC gain yielded conditional 95% interval **[+0.0074,+0.0284]**, yet **3 of 30 circular ephemeris-shift placebo models** had AUC >= actual, empirical plus-one p≈**0.129**. Exploratory/multiple-hypothesis model search, NOT proof of an astrology-specific forecast edge.
- Nodal AUC yearly split: **2025 0.51552 vs 0.50624 baseline** and **2026 0.51555 vs 0.51425 baseline**; pooled AUC differential overstates within-year consistent incremental gains.
- All models trained only through 2024; 2025-26 predictions were held back from *this training*, though those market years were used previously in other research. No next-period prospective verification yet. First/last H1 tick fills are idealized and cannot be reproduced perfectly in real trading; no live slippage, swaps, account HMR.
